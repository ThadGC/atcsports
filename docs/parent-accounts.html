Parent accounts, registration and the logged‑in area

What a parent does on the new site, what the league gets from it, and the handful of decisions still waiting on ATC.

Prepared by Apex Growth For ATC Sports, Roseville CA

The short version

A parent creates one account for their family. Inside it they add each child they want to play, choose the division, and note who the child wants to be on a team with. Payment and official league registration still happen on TeamLinkt, the system ATC already uses.

The account is the part that is new. It gives parents somewhere to keep their family's details between seasons instead of retyping them, and it gives ATC a record of who is interested before anyone has paid.

What a parent actually does

  1. 01

    Taps Register

    From the top of any page, the button in the hero, or the bar that follows them up the page on a phone.

  2. 02

    Creates the account

    First name, last name, email and a password. Mobile number is optional and the form says why it is asked for: weather calls and game day changes.

    No card details at this stage. Four fields, about thirty seconds. If they mistype a common email domain the form offers the correction before they submit.

  3. 03

    Lands in their account

    They are logged in straight away rather than being sent back to a login screen. The page greets them by first name and shows an empty athlete list.

  4. 04

    Adds each child

    Name, division picked from the nine ATC runs, and a free text box for a friend or coach request. One account holds as many children as the family has playing.

  5. 05

    Goes to TeamLinkt to pay

    The account page carries a button through to the existing registration portal. The spot is confirmed once that is done, and the page says so in those words.

  6. 06

    Comes back whenever

    The login lasts a week on that device. Returning parents add another child, drop one who is not playing this season, or check their division's Saturday time.

What the account holds

Deliberately little. Everything here is something a parent would expect to give a youth league, and nothing is collected that the site does not use.

How accounts are protected

Passwords are put through a one way scramble before they are saved, so nobody at ATC or at Apex Growth can read a parent's password, and a stolen copy of the database does not hand anyone a working login.

The login itself is kept in a cookie the browser will not let page scripts read, which is the protection against a common form of session theft. If someone enters an email that has no account, the site gives the same message as a wrong password, so the login page cannot be used to work out which families are registered. Repeated failed attempts from the same place are slowed down automatically.

One family cannot see or delete another family's children. Every request for athlete records is checked against the account that is signed in.

The contact form

Messages sent from the contact page are saved with the sender's name, email, subject and message. The form validates before it sends and confirms on screen when it has gone through, so nobody is left wondering.

Those messages are currently stored rather than emailed, because ATC has not picked an email provider yet. That is one of the open decisions below.

What is running, and what ATC still needs to decide

Accounts, athletes and the contact form

Working

Signing up, signing in, adding and removing children, and sending a message all work today.

Where the data lives

Needs ATC

Records currently sit in a file on the server, which is fine for a demonstration but not for a live season. Connecting a proper database is a configuration change, not a rebuild. ATC picks the host.

Sending email

Needs ATC

Nothing is emailed yet: no welcome message, and no notification to the league when a contact form comes in. This needs an email provider account in ATC's name.

Forgotten passwords

Not built

There is no self service password reset, because a reset has to arrive by email. It follows on from the email decision above, and should be in place before a real season opens.

A league view of all parents

Not built

ATC cannot currently log in and browse every family. The permission levels for parent, coach and admin already exist in the system, but no screen has been built on top of them. Worth deciding whether ATC wants this or is happy staying in TeamLinkt.

Payment

Stays on TeamLinkt

No change proposed. Fees, refunds and official registration continue exactly as they do now.

Suggested order

Pick an email provider first, because the welcome message, the contact notification and the password reset all depend on it. Connect the database next. The league view can wait until ATC has used the site for a season and knows whether it is needed.
