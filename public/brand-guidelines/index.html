01

Positioning Proposed

Everything in this section is a recommendation from Apex Growth, drawn from how ATC already presents itself. It is not yet client approved. Every other section in this document records something that already exists.

The promise

Competitive football that respects the family's Saturday. Practice and game run back to back on one field, one morning, so a season costs a parent nine mornings rather than a rearranged week.

The tension it resolves

Parents are told they must choose between a serious program and a manageable one. ATC's format refuses that trade. Real turf, real officials, ten player rosters.

Earned

Nothing is claimed that the league cannot deliver on a Saturday.

Direct

Plain sentences. A parent gets the answer without decoding it.

Competitive

The tagline is Built To Compete. The brand never softens into participation language.

Local

Roseville and Rocklin by name. Whitney High and Mahany Park, not "our venues".

02

Logo and wordmark

The mark is the ATC monogram in metallic gold with a hard italic thrust, followed by SPORTS in flat white. It always sits on a dark ground. There is no approved light ground version.

Clear space

Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the S in SPORTS. In the site header the mark sits at 24px to 32px tall depending on breakpoint.

Minimum size

Do not reproduce below 96px wide. Below that the monogram's inner counters close up and the metallic gradient reads as flat brown.

Never

Do not recolour the monogram flat gold, place it on white, add a drop shadow, stretch it, or separate ATC from SPORTS.

A practical note

The supplied wide PNG carried 235px of transparent padding on its left edge, about a fifth of the file. Any new export must be alpha trimmed before use or it will never optically align.

03

Colour

Two families only. A near black ink that carries everything, and a single gold used as the accent. There is no third brand colour, and adding one is the fastest way to make an ATC surface stop looking like ATC.

Ink#050505 Page ground. The default everywhere.
Ink 800#0A0A0B Raised panels, footer.
Ink 700#101012 Cards, menu grounds.
Ink 600#17171A Inputs, table stripes.
Gold Lite#F0DCA8 Eyebrow labels, small caps on dark.
Gold#D9B86C The accent. Primary buttons, rules, icons.
Gold 500#C6A055 Gradient mid tone, pressed states.
Gold 700#7C6335 Section numbers, dim marks. Never body text.

The metal gradient

Display headings use a five stop gradient clipped to the text, never a flat gold:

168deg · #F7ECD0 0% · #D9B86C 26% · #A8874A 52% · #E8D29A 74% · #C6A055 100%

The light stop at 74 percent is what reads as a specular highlight. Removing it makes the type look muddy.

Contrast

Gold #D9B86C on ink #050505 passes comfortably for body and large text. The reverse, ink on gold, is the pairing used for primary buttons and is the only approved inversion.

Gold 700 is a structural colour. It fails contrast as body text and must stay on numerals, rules and dim marks.

05

Surfaces and motion

Bezel

The card treatment. A 6px outer tray carrying a gold tinted gradient, wrapping an inner plate. Radius 2rem. It reads as machined metal, not as a drop shadow.

Glass

The floating navigation bar. Ground at 72 percent opacity with blur(22px) saturate(150%). Menu panels go to 97 percent, because a dropdown over live content has to stay readable.

Stadium glow

Three radial gradients of gold at 20, 12 and 8 percent, placed top left, top right and bottom centre. It suggests floodlights without ever showing one.

Easing

Everything moves on cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1), typically 500ms to 700ms. It starts fast and settles slowly. A spring curve exists for playful moments and is used sparingly.

Never

Do not animate a decorative pseudo element with translate(100%). Sweeps animate background-position. A single stray keyframe of this kind once added 1350px of horizontal scroll to every page on the site.

06

Photography

Every image is a real ATC Saturday. No stock, no staged studio work, no composites of children who never played in this league.

What to shoot

The moment of contest: a flag being pulled, a receiver securing a catch, a quarterback setting his feet. Coaches belong in frame, on the sideline, watching.

Treatment

Warm afternoon light, deep shadows, the black and gold kit carrying the colour. Images sit under a black wash of 82 to 90 percent when used as a backdrop, so they read as depth rather than as a photograph.

Cropping

A phone hero fits a wide photo by height, so it crops sideways only. Vertical framing has to be solved in the source file, not with CSS.

Never

No promotional flyers in a gallery of game day photography. No child identifiable by name alongside their image without a parent's consent.

07

The uniform

The kit is the brand's most seen surface. It appears on a field every Saturday for nine weeks, photographed by parents, long before anyone visits a web page.

As worn

Black jersey with gold trim at the V neck and shoulder, the ATC SPORTS wordmark across the chest, and a large white number. Black shorts carry the wordmark on the thigh. The flag belt is gold, which makes the defining moment of the sport, the pull, the most visible thing in any photograph.

How it is sold

A lightweight premium reversible jersey with customisable name and number. It is a separate one time purchase, reusable across future seasons, which keeps league fees down. First season registration includes it, a stated saving of $45.

08

Voice

ATC talks to a parent who is already busy and already paying. The voice is confident and unhurried, and it never oversells, because the format is the argument.

Write this way

"Practice runs the hour before your kid's game, on the same field, same morning. No weeknight driving, no second trip across town."

Not this way

"We are passionate about empowering young athletes to unlock their full potential through our world class developmental pathway."

Address the parent

Second person. "You are the one driving, paying and rearranging the weekend." The athlete is "your kid", not "your athlete".

Lead with the constraint

Nine Saturdays. Ten players. Two fields. Numbers first, adjectives second, and only adjectives the numbers have already earned.

Two hard rules

No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, in any ATC copy. And never state a fact about the league that ATC has not published. If it cannot be delivered on a Saturday, it does not go on the page.

09

Components

Buttons

Fully rounded. Gold fill for the primary action, a hairline ghost for the secondary. Each carries a circular pip holding an arrow or icon, which shifts 4px right and 1px up on hover. Content is always centred, so a stretched button never hugs its left edge.

Minimum target

Every interactive element is at least 44px tall. This is enforced by an automated sweep of the built site rather than left to review.

Navigation

A floating glass bar, centre aligned, that tightens as the page scrolls. Below the large breakpoint it collapses to a full screen panel. The logo returns to the top of the home page from anywhere.

Sticky action bar

On phones a Register and Call us pair pins to the bottom edge, appearing only once the hero's own buttons have scrolled away, so the two never compete.

Winter 2026 season

Saturdays from 14 November 2026 to 9 January 2027. Week 3 falls on Thanksgiving weekend, week 7 is a bye for Christmas, and week 9 is the championship. Games run rain or shine unless conditions force a postponement.

Divisions

Co-ed from Pre-K and Kindergarten through 7th and 8th grade, five bands. Girls from 1st and 2nd through 7th and 8th grade, four bands. Each team practises the hour before its own game.

Fields
